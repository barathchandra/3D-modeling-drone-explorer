Upload 3D models here (GLB format).

Required for component explorer:
  propeller.glb — Propellers slide
  motor.glb    — Motors slide
  arm.glb      — Arms slide
  battery.glb  — Battery slide
  fc.glb       — Flight Controller slide
  pixhawk.glb  — Pixhawk slide
  pdb.glb      — Power Distribution Board slide
  esc.glb      — ESC slide (large file)
  remote.glb   — Remote Controller slide
  stand.glb    — Landing Gear slide
  frame.glb    — Airframe slide
  overview-textured.glb — Overview (from textured_mesh.glb)
  camera.glb   — Camera/Gimbal slide
  gp.glb       — GPS Module slide

After copying files, restart: npm run dev

Files are served at /models/your-file.glb
